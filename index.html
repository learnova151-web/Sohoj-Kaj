Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, trial or card. Offline mode costs nothing to run. Cloud mode runs on your own Google Gemini key: Google offers a free tier with daily limits, and anything beyond that is billed by Google directly to you, not by me.

Which computers does it run on?

Any Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) or an Intel chip, running macOS 10.15 or newer, plus 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that safe?

Windows shows that warning for new apps it has not seen often enough yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. On Mac, the app is signed and verified by Apple.

Where do my recordings go?

In offline mode, nowhere at all: everything stays on your machine. In cloud mode, each recording is sent to Google for transcription using your key. I never see your recordings or your text.

Does it work in every app?

Everywhere you can type: Facebook, WhatsApp, Gmail, Word, Google Docs, the browser. Shobdo types the text right where your cursor sits.

Can I change the keys?

Yes, from Settings. By default Bangla is Right Command and English is Fn on Mac, while Windows uses Right Alt and Right Ctrl.
